Questions?
We've got answers

What is Roofus?
Roofus is a teeth-cleaning wipe for dogs. It slides onto your finger. One side is bumpy to scrub. The other side is smooth to clean the gums. Each can has 60 wipes.
Who made it?
Dr. Nicole Leibman, a vet, made the formula.
How do I use it?
Put a wipe on your finger. Rub the teeth with the bumpy side. Flip it and wipe the gums with the smooth side. Throw it away. It takes about 30 seconds.
How often should I use it?
2 or 3 times a week. You can use it every day if you like.
What if my dog won't let me?
Go slow. Night 1, let them lick it. Night 2, clean the front teeth. Add a few more teeth each time. The peanut butter taste helps.
Is it safe if my dog licks it?
Yes. It has no alcohol, no xylitol, no parabens, no dyes and no added scent.
Does it replace the vet?
No. If your dog has hard brown tartar or sore gums, see your vet first. Roofus is for home care between vet visits.
