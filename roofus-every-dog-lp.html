Frequently asked
questions

What is Roofus?
Roofus Dental Finger Wipes are dual-sided wipes that slide onto your finger like a sleeve. One side is beaded to scrub, the other is smooth to polish. Each canister holds 60 wipes in a peanut butter flavor.
Who is Roofus for?
Any dog with teeth, and any owner who has tried a brush and lost. It's gentle enough for puppies, seniors and toy breeds.
How do I use it?
Slide a wipe onto your finger, scrub the teeth with the beaded side, flip and polish along the gums with the smooth side, then toss it. About 30 seconds.
How often should I use it?
Two or three times a week is the routine most owners keep. Daily is fine too.
What if my dog won't let me?
Start slow. Let them lick the wipe on night one, do the front teeth on night two, and work back from there. The peanut butter flavor helps.
Is it safe if my dog licks it?
Yes. The formula is plant-based and has no alcohol, xylitol, parabens, dyes or added fragrance.
Does it replace a vet cleaning?
No. If your dog already has heavy tartar or sore gums, see your vet first. Roofus is the at-home routine between checkups.
